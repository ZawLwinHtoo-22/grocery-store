How to Order & Track — User Guide

This guide explains the order lifecycle, payment, and tracking flow for customers.

  1. Choose Products
    Browse products, add desired quantities to your cart.
  2. Checkout & Payment
    Fill in your name, phone, delivery address and optionally upload your payment screenshot (or you can upload it after placing order on the tracking page).
  3. Order Created (OD-XXXXX)
    After placing an order you receive a tracking code like OD-00001. Save it or bookmark the tracking link.
  4. Payment Verification
    Our team reviews the payment screenshot. If approved, your order moves to Processing.
  5. Out for Delivery
    When the order is dispatched, status updates to Out for Delivery.
  6. Delivered
    Once delivered the order status will show Completed.
Tracking your order

Open the Track page and enter your tracking code or phone number. You can also use the date filters to find recent orders.

Admin actions

Admins verify payments, mark orders Processing, Out for Delivery, and Completed. Contact support if you believe your order was handled incorrectly.
